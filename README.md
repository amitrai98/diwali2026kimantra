# 🪔 Sikka Kimantra Greens Residents - Diwali 2026 Staff Appreciation Drive

An interactive festive web tribute and social media video suite celebrating and honoring the security, housekeeping, and maintenance heroes of **Sikka Kimantra Greens** (Maurya and Kiyaan Towers) during Diwali 2026.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Cinematic Photo Slideshow**: 16 heartwarming celebration moments captured during the appreciation drive with Ken Burns animations and ambient lighting.
- **Background Music**: Autoplays the festive track *"Shaam-e-Rangoli..."* in a continuous loop with browser-policy smart fallback and animated equalizer waves.
- **14 Contributing Resident Families**:
  - Interactive floating resident bubbles at the bottom with real-time moment spotlighting.
  - Tower filtering (**All**, **Maurya Tower**, **Kiyaan Tower**).
  - Modal roster detailing all 14 families and apartment numbers.
- **Confetti & Sparklers**: Interactive Diwali fireworks and particle effects.
- **Video Downloads**:
  - 📱 **Portrait Reel (9:16 / 1080×1920)**: Formatted for Instagram Reels, Stories, and WhatsApp Status (`Diwali_2026_Staff_Appreciation_Reels_9x16.mp4`).
  - 🎬 **Landscape Video (16:9 / 1920×1080)**: Full HD widescreen video for feeds and TVs (`Diwali_2026_Staff_Appreciation.mp4`).
- **Offline Standalone Version**: `diwali_slideshow_standalone.html` bundles all photos and the complete audio track embedded directly as base64 for offline sharing via AirDrop or WhatsApp.
